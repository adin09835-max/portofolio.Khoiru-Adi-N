01 / About

Working across the electrical stack

Khoiru Adi Nugroho is an Electrical Engineering student at Universitas Muhammadiyah Surakarta with hands-on project experience spanning IoT, embedded systems, automation, MEP, electrical installation, and solar photovoltaic systems. He is comfortable working across the full lifecycle of a project — from programming ESP32-based sensor systems and integrating IoT hardware, to reading and applying MEP technical drawings on an active construction site.

His project work includes an EV charging station (SPKLU) monitoring system, an automatic IoT-based irrigation system, an MEP internship on a university building project, and a final-year project on dual-axis solar panel optimization using machine learning. He works carefully and analytically across cross-disciplinary projects, coordinating with teams to resolve technical issues both in the field and in system development.
